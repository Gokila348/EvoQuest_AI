⏳ THE LOOP HAS BEGUN...

You have 10 seconds per loop. Every action you perform is remembered by your Past Self.

When the loop resets, your Past Self repeats your actions. Stand on a button and stay there until time runs out, then your Past Self holds it for you while you walk through the gate. Lasers are deadly. Each gate has its own button (A, B, C, D). Collect the gold coins (+10). From level 4 a Sentinel AI hunts you: it studies the places you favour in earlier loops and heads there, so change your routes! Buttons are safe zones.
